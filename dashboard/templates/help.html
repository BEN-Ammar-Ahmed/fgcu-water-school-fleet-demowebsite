{{ icon('search', 16) }} Searching

  • On the home page, click the round search button (or press / anywhere) and type. Press Enter to search, or Esc to close.
  • You can search for a vehicle name, property tag, plate, VIN or hull number, a driver, a destination, a vendor or a document type.
  • Dates work too: 2026-10-03, 10/03/2026, October 2026 or just October.
  • The buttons under the bar (All, Vehicles, Trips, Fuel, Maintenance, Documents) narrow the results. After picking one, a row of filters appears, for example vehicle, driver and date range for trips.
  • Each group shows up to 50 results, newest first. The heading shows the full count, so narrow the search if you need the rest.

{{ icon('plus', 16) }} Adding records (Tier 2 · can edit and up)

  • Use Add in the top bar for a vehicle, trip, fuel purchase, service or driver. On a vehicle's page, each section also has its own add button, which fills in the vehicle for you.
  • Trips can only use approved, active drivers. A trip without an end time is shown as "In progress".
  • Trips, fuel and services need the vehicle to have a property tag. Add one on the vehicle's Edit page first.
  • Logging a service with the same name as a schedule item (for example Oil Change) moves that schedule forward.
  • Every change is recorded in the database's audit log, with the time and the old or new values.

{{ icon('admin', 16) }} Getting access

Sign in with your FGCU account. If the site says you don't have access yet, ask the fleet manager (or another admin) to add you and choose your level.
